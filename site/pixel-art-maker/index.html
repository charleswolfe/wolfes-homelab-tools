Pixel Art Maker

A retro paint program for pixel art: draw pixel by pixel with a pencil, straight lines and an eraser in the classic 16 colours or any colour, then save it as a BMP. No ads, no accounts and nothing leaves your device, so it’s safe for kids too.

Canvas and file

Tips: hold Shift with the line tool to snap to 45°, Alt-click to pick up a colour, and use P, L, E and G for pencil, line, eraser and grid. Background is the colour of the next new canvas, and the colour the eraser paints on it: BMP files have no transparency. New canvas and Open image replace the drawing (undo brings it back).
